🔐 What Data We Access & Why

When you authorize ADs-LearyClaw through Google OAuth, we request access to the following:

✅

Google Ads Data

Campaign performance, keywords, search terms, and ad group metrics — used to generate reports and optimize your campaigns.

✅

Google Account Info

Your name and email address — used solely for API authentication and account identification.

What we do NOT do:

  • ❌ We do not store your data long-term — data is processed in real-time during sessions
  • ❌ We do not share or sell your data to any third party
  • ❌ We do not access your Google account beyond the scope of your explicit requests
  • ❌ We do not use your data for any purpose other than campaign analysis and optimization

⚙️ How It Works

  1. Authorize via Google OAuth — connect your Google Ads account in seconds
  2. Talk naturally — ask questions like "How did my campaigns perform last week?" or "Find me high-volume keywords for car insurance"
  3. Get insights — receive structured reports with actionable recommendations
  4. Take action — request changes like adjusting budgets, pausing underperforming ads, or adding negative keywords
